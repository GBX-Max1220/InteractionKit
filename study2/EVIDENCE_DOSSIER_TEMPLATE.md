## Audit checklist

- [ ] Two or more authoritative sources are recorded and metadata-verified.
- [ ] Each source is linked to both the binary decision and support-level claim.
- [ ] Two independent domain reviews are complete.
- [ ] Reviewers agree on the binary decision and support level, or adjudication is complete.
- [ ] Decision boundary and numerical granularity are explicit.
- [ ] No pilot answer, scenario wording, or condition label reveals the intended taxonomy.
- [ ] Registry record and dossier final decision agree.
